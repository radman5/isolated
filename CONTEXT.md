# ISOLA

## Language

**Route**:
The stretch of hostile ground between two settlements. It is either Closed or Open, and once opened it stays open.
_Avoid_: level, dungeon, road (road is fine in dialogue)

**Leg**:
One half of a route, from an entrance or rest point to the next rest point or the far settlement. Health carries through a leg.

**Rest point**:
The single place in the middle of a route that fully restores health.

**Attrition**:
The rule that health never regenerates on its own, so what one fight costs you, you carry into the next one in the same leg.
