# Beyond the Glade

## Language

**The Glade**:
The home settlement, where the orphanage stands and where recruits come to live. "Home" is its role: the place you return to between routes.
_Avoid_: Home as a proper name, Settlement 1

**Route**:
The stretch of hostile ground between two settlements. It is either Closed or Open, and once opened it stays open.
_Avoid_: level, dungeon, road (road is fine in dialogue)

**Leg**:
One half of a route, from an entrance or rest point to the next rest point or the far settlement. Health carries through a leg, and dying sends you back to the start of the leg you died in.

**Rest point**:
The single place in the middle of a route that fully restores health. On Route 1 it is a waystone.
_Avoid_: checkpoint, save point

**Attrition**:
The rule that health never regenerates on its own, so what one fight costs you, you carry into the next one in the same leg.

**Stealth stretch**:
A section of a route built to be sneaked through, past monsters too strong to fight. If you're caught, you flee rather than fight.
_Avoid_: stealth level, stealth section

**Kit**:
The weapons and skills the player has at a given point in the game. It grows as the game goes on, through gifts, recruits and settlement NPCs. On Route 1 the kit is the sword and the dodge.
_Avoid_: loadout, skills, abilities

**Recruit**:
A person you convince to move to the Glade once a route to them is open. There are five in the whole game, and each is won over by a single convincing act.
_Avoid_: companion (unless they travel with you), party member, follower

**Convincing act**:
The one specific thing that wins a recruit over, lasting a few minutes, not a quest chain. Each kind is used once in the game.
_Avoid_: recruitment quest

**Skill**:
A technique an NPC teaches you, such as the charged chain. It has its own level, which rises through use, and its own cooldown.
_Avoid_: ability, perk, spell (the player never has magic)

**Cooldown**:
The wait before a skill or the dodge can be used again. It is the only limit on actions: there is no stamina.
_Avoid_: stamina, energy, mana

**Character level**:
The player's overall level. It rises with experience earned mostly from milestones (opening a route, winning a recruit, key fights) and a little from kills, and it raises max health. It is separate from skill levels.
_Avoid_: rank, XP level
